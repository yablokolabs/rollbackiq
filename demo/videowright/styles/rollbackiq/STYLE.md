# Style: RollbackIQ

Videos about RollbackIQ: the demonstration video and walkthroughs.

- Light frame, navy text, one blue accent. Green is a measurement inside its limit, red is
  a breach or a refusal, amber is a caution or a wait for a person.
- Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for identifiers, job names, hashes and measured values.
- A screenshot is evidence. It is the page as GitLab or the incident console served it, in
  a frame, with an `alt` text that says what it shows. It is not edited.
- Every number on screen comes from an artifact in `evidence/`. A scene that shows a limit
  of the evidence says so on screen.
- Motion: elements rise and fade in, 500 ms. No other animation.
